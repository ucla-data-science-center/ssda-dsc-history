I'm writing an institutional history of UCLA's social science data archive (founded 1961; the Social Science Data Archive from 1977; part of the UCLA Library's Data Science Center, "DSC," from 2018). The page is public: https://ucla-data-science-center.github.io/ssda-dsc-history/history.html. I directed the unit from 2017 through 2026, so I need an outside check on a claim I'm making from experience. Please browse and verify; don't answer from memory.

## The claim

After 2017 the unit stopped publishing new studies under the archive's own name, but it kept curating other people's research data. Through trial and error, not by plan, a two-stage pattern emerged:

1. A UCLA research group works its dataset out and publishes it in UCLA Dataverse (dataverse.ucla.edu, DOI prefix 10.25346), with DSC staff helping shape it.
2. Once the data is stable, it goes to ICPSR (DOI prefix 10.3886), which has the staff to curate to the variable level and document it for long-term reuse.

## Evidence I have

- UCLA Law COVID Behind Bars Data Project: *COVID Prison Policy Index* in UCLA Dataverse, January 2022 (doi:10.25346/S6/B5GOLX). ICPSR released *COVID Behind Bars: COVID Prison Policy Index, United States, 2020-2021* as study 38730 in 2023 (doi:10.3886/ICPSR38730.v1). DSC scheduling records show a May 2022 consultation where the project asked "Would you recommend depositing to Open ICPSR?" and "Do you recommend paying for curation?"
- Carceral Ecologies (Nicholas Shapiro): *Autopsy Analysis of Deaths in LA County Jail, 2009-2018* in UCLA Dataverse, May 2023 (doi:10.25346/S6/JOOUYM). ICPSR study 38958, February 2024 (doi:10.3886/ICPSR38958.v1). DSC records show an "Open ICPSR documentation conversation" in June 2021 and "Discuss data submission to ICPSR" in October 2022.
- Williams Institute (UCLA School of Law): TransPop (ICPSR 37938), Generations (ICPSR 37166), a combined file (ICPSR 38421), and Mapping LGBTQ Equality (ICPSR 37877) are at ICPSR. My account is that DSC supported that curation. The Williams Institute's only UCLA Dataverse dataset (LGBT health services, 2021, doi:10.25346/S6/N3XTZB) has no ICPSR counterpart I could find, so Williams may not fit the two-stage pattern.
- BioCritical Studies Lab (Terence Keel): three 2025 datasets in UCLA Dataverse, including *Autopsies from The Coroner's Silence* (doi:10.25346/S6R7CHCI) and *LA County In-Custody Death Data, 2008-2019* (doi:10.25346/S627C42Q). Not at ICPSR. Keel's data is compiled from multiple sources.
