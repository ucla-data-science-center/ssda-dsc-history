# DSC-era people: Doug Daniels, Todd Grappone, Tim Dennis, Jamie Jamison

Sources pulled 2026-07-31, prompted by Tim asking to credit specific people. Mix of
UCLA Library staff pages, UCLA Newsroom, The Carpentries, and Dataverse Community
Meeting materials.

## Doug Daniels

- UCLA Library staff page: https://www.library.ucla.edu/about/staff/doug-daniels/
- UCLA Newsroom, "Doug Daniels and Matthew Vest named UCLA's 2026 Librarians of the
  Year": https://newsroom.ucla.edu/stories/doug-daniels-matthew-vest--ucla-librarians-of-the-year-2026

Joined UCLA Library as staff in 2014 (BA History, UCLA). Completed an MLIS at UCLA
in 2020 and moved into the librarian faculty track and onto the DSC team that same
year, per Tim directly. Title: Emerging Technologies Librarian. Manages the Lux Lab
(3D printing/scanning, laser etching/cutting, large-format printing, VR/XR content
development), and does drone mapping/imaging work, including a survey of Iceland's
Mosfell Valley with a Cotsen Institute of Archaeology doctoral student. Also
developed a surgical probe holder with an undergraduate, now in use at Ronald
Reagan UCLA Medical Center, and co-organized Little Saigon @ UCLA: International
Digital Archaeology Day.

**Award**: 2026 Librarian of the Year Award, LAUC-LA (Librarians Association of the
University of California, Los Angeles chapter), honored at a June 4, 2026
ceremony. Not "Distinguished Librarian" as recalled; that's the actual award name.
Timing matches "won it a month ago" relative to 2026-07-31 closely enough.

## Todd Grappone

- ARL announcement: https://www.arl.org/news/todd-grappone-named-university-librarian-for-uc-santa-barbara/
- UCSB Library: https://www.library.ucsb.edu/news/grappone-appointed-ul

AUL (Associate University Librarian) at UCLA from 2010, serving multiple roles
including AUL for Digital Initiatives and Information Technology (DIIT) and, from
2023, interim AUL for Collections, the Data Science Center, and Resource
Acquisitions & Metadata Services. Prior experience at Stanford Medicine, USC, and
Occidental College. At UCLA, also developed the Modern Endangered Archives
Program, the International Digital Ephemera Project, and the Sinai Palimpsest
Project. Per Tim: shepherded SSDA's transfer into the Library (2014), coined the
"Data Science Center" name, and helped design/reorient the 2018 service model.
None of this last part independently verified in public sources yet; it's Tim's
direct testimony as a participant, which is exactly the kind of thing oral history
is for.

Appointed University Librarian at UC Santa Barbara, effective July 1, 2025.

## Tim Dennis

- The Carpentries, "2018 Community Service Award Winners": https://carpentries.org/blog/2018/12/community-service-award/

Won The Carpentries' **2018 Community Service Award** (not "Community Member
Award" as recalled; that's the actual name), announced December 10, 2018, shared
with Margareth Gfrerer and Toby Hodges. Citation: "Director, UCLA Libraries Social
Sciences Data Archive... certified Instructor since 2016 for organizing and
teaching over 20 workshops, bridging The Carpentries with libraries, establishing
Library Carpentry as a Lesson Program, and mentoring trainees."

## Jamie (Jina) Jamison

- UCLA Library staff page: https://www.library.ucla.edu/about/staff/jina-jamie-jamison/
- Dataverse Community Meeting 2021: https://projects.iq.harvard.edu/dcm2021/people/jamie-jamison

Official title: Collection Manager, Data Science Center. Listed expertise:
"Carpentries, Data Discovery, Data Management, Dataverse, Research Data
Management, SPSS." Spoke/chaired at the Dataverse Community Meeting 2021,
representing UCLA Library. Author of UCLA Dataverse preservation documentation
(jmjamison.github.io/preservation.html), consistent with a lead role in the
Dataverse implementation, though no source found so far states "lead" as a formal
title. Tim's claim that Jamison's UCLA tenure goes back to the 1980s is not yet
independently corroborated by any public source; it's Tim's own recollection, and
consistent with (though more specific than) the 2014 MOU's framing of Jamison as
continuing staff with no break in service.
