# Elizabeth (Libbie) Stephenson, vitae

Source: `stephenson-vitae.pdf` (converted from "Elizabeth Stephenson.vitae.final rev.docx," supplied by Tim Dennis 2026-09-25). Undated; latest entries read "2010-," so roughly 2010-2014.

## Key facts

* Title line: "Distinguished Librarian / Director, Social Science Data Archive, University of California, Los Angeles." Section heading: "Social Science Data Archivist."
* "I have been manager and director of the UCLA Social Science Data Archive since 1977." This is earlier than the "at least 1986" in `dsc-evidence-registry/claims/dennis-2017-director-succession.yaml`, which should now cite this document.
* Mission language matches the SSDA website (see `ssda-website-wayback-2015-2016.md`): "management of research output throughout the entire life cycle of a project"; ICPSR and Roper memberships used by faculty in courses.
* Professional service: IASSIST President 1995-1997, IASSIST Quarterly editor 1982-1984, chair of membership (1980-82, 1990-92) and privacy/human subjects (2008-); ICPSR Governing Council 1996-2000, Archival Development Committee 1996-2000, organizational representative 1978-, William H. Flanigan Award for Distinguished Service 2007, IMLS and Teaching with Data advisory committees; Roper Center board 2010-2012; APDU/Census SIPP working group chair 1992-1995; NHGIS advisory group 2001-2005; UCLA ITPB research data management subcommittee 2008-2010, IDRE-HASIS 2008-.
* Education: BA Fine Arts, UC Riverside 1975; MLIS, UCLA 1977.

## Caveat

The website contact page (Feb 2015) lists her as "Archivist"; the vitae uses both "Director" and "Archivist." Both are her own usage.
