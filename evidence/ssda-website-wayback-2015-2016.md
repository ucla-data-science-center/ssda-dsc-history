## Key facts

* History paragraph (home page): data services at UCLA since 1961 (Marvick's Political Behavior Archive; UCLA among the 8 founding members of ICPSR, 1962); 1964 merger with the Survey Research Center's planned Archive Data Library under Eve Fielder; 1977 new framework designed by Donald Treiman; later development under William A.V. Clark. Note: this text says "merged," matching the September 2017 deck, not the November deck's "joined" (see `dsc-evidence-registry/claims/ssda-1961-founding.yaml`).
* Mission (home page): faculty support across "an entire research project," partnership "at all stages of the research process," "long term preservation of research results," and instructional support.
* Staff (contact page, Feb 2015): Libbie Stephenson, Archivist; Jamie Jamison, Technology Specialist. Location 1120-H Rolfe Hall.
* Navigation lists deposit options: SSDA, ICPSR, Dataverse, UCLA Library, California Digital Library.
* By January 2016 the site redirected to the SSDA collection in Harvard Dataverse. Complements `ssda-harvard-dataverse-about.md`.
