## Doug Daniels — full career record, per his CV

**UCLA Library career timeline** (precisely dated, four roles):
- Helpdesk Specialist — September 2013 – June 2014
- Technical Projects Specialist — June 2014 – August 2016 (built UCLA Library's first 3D
  printing services; developed lending film-production kits)
- **Coordinator of Scholarly Innovation Technologies** — August 2016 – April 2020
- **Emerging Technologies Librarian** — April 2020 – present

Doug's UCLA Library tenure starts **2013**, four years before Tim's 2017 hire and five years
before the 2018 DSC rebrand — his own career is itself a continuity thread spanning the
pre-DSC/post-DSC boundary, independent of the archive-specific continuity argument already
made via `ucla-data-archive_old`.

**Education**: MLIS, Informatics track, UCLA, June 2020; BA History (German minor), UCLA, 2014,
Summa Cum Laude, Departmental Highest Honors.

**The physical/service brand**: "**Lux Lab**" — the named UCLA Library emerging-technology
lab/service Doug has run since 2016 (3D printing, scanning, laser etching/cutting, large-format
printing, VR/AR, drone). Not previously documented anywhere in this project.

**Certified Drone Pilot** — confirms the drone work directly, not just inferred from project
descriptions.

## Professional leadership — real, current, international

- **3D Data UC Common Knowledge Group** — Co-founder and Chair, 2020–present. This is the same

  (`ucla-data-science-center/3DAtlas`, "created and maintained by the UC Common Knowledge Group
  on 3D Data") — Doug co-founded and chairs it.

## Selected 3D scanning/capture projects (partial list, from the CV)

Spans UCLA and beyond: Fowler Museum (Virgin Mary statuette, Vietnamese ceramics, South Pacific
artifacts, African ceremonial blades), UCLA Anatomy Lab (cadaver dissection stages), Semel
Institute (orbital septums), LACMA (Ancient Egyptian sarcophagus lids), UCLA Library Special
Collections cuneiform tablets (Clark, Dickson, Cotsen collections), the Clark Library (building
and cultural heritage objects), the Getty Center and Museo Egizio in Turin, Italy (Egyptian
artifacts, with Cotsen Institute, July-August 2018), UCSD Scripps Institution of Oceanography
(the FLIP research vessel, LiDAR/structured-light scans, Aug 2021), UCSD Birch Aquarium (marine
life specimens, June 2021).

**Directly relevant to this project**: a **terrestrial LiDAR scan of Powell Library's exterior
and interior**, Nov-Dec 2021, "for preservation, documentation, and exhibition of 3D data
tools" — Powell Library, the building named for the same Powell family (Norman Powell) whose
gifts fund DSC's data scientist position, deep learning machine, and DataSquad seed funding
(see `evidence/powell-gifts-and-imls-grant.md`). Independent confirmation the Powell
relationship runs deeper than funding alone.

## Direct link to Norman Powell's ongoing patronage

Doug's CV lists a **recurring "Powell Society Open House Demo"** — Young Research Library,
invited past and prospective donors, **March 2019, 2018, and 2017**. This means Doug's
emerging-technology work was actively showcased to the Powell family/Powell Society donor
circle in the years immediately following Tim's 2017 hire — direct evidence connecting Doug's
work to the donor-relations side of the Powell funding story, not just a coincidence of naming.
His CV also states he "work[s] closely with Library Development to identify and steward donors,
collections, and endowed gift funds" as a standing job duty.

## Pandemic-era contribution

**"3DP PPE @ UCLA: Making it Through a Pandemic"** — a July 1, 2020 talk describing 3D-printed
PPE production at UCLA during COVID-19. A concrete, dated example of the Lux Lab's emerging-tech
capacity being redirected to a public-health need — worth a mention alongside the archive's own
COVID-adjacent work (UCLA Law COVID Behind Bars, hosted in Dataverse, see
`evidence/dsc-era-hosted-collections.md`) as a 2020 through-line.
