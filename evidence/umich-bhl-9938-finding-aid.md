# Bentley Historical Library finding aid: ICPSR records, 1962-2007

Source: `umich-bhl-9938.pdf`, downloaded by Tim 2026-07-31. University of Michigan
Bentley Historical Library finding aid for "Inter-University Consortium for Political
and Social Research records, 1962-2007" (call number 9938 Bimu 2, 21 linear feet).
Six series: Organization and Administration; Meetings; Individual Council Chairs'
Papers; Resource Development and Services; Grants and Project Proposals; Educational
Activities.

This is a finding aid (an index of box/folder contents), not the documents themselves.
It tells you what to request, not what's in the files. Read the first 40 of 53 pages;
the rest is the Educational Activities series, judged lower-priority for the founding
question and not read yet.

## Folders most relevant to Marvick's founding-era role

- **Council Meetings, Meeting Materials, 1966 and 1967** — the actual minutes from the
  two years Marvick chaired the ICPR Council, per Eulau's memoir. The single most
  direct lead in this collection for confirming or expanding his documented role.
- **Committee of Eight, Correspondence and Minutes, 1962-1963** (Box 1) — founding-year
  committee records. Not yet clear whether "Committee of Eight" is the same body as the
  "council of five representatives" or "Committee of Representatives" mentioned in
  Miller's 1962 memo (`miller-1962-considerations.md`) — worth checking directly.
- **Social Science Research Council Committee on American Electoral Behavior,
  1961-1962** (Box 3) — a precursor committee active the exact year the archive was
  founded. Possible source for who was involved before ICPR's own structure existed.
- **Formation of a Data Repository, 1962** (Box 14) — title suggests founding-era
  decision-making correspondence.
- **Memorandum of Organization, 1962** (Box 3) — a specific dated copy of the founding
  document; worth comparing against Tim's own `Miller-Likert.pdf` to see if it's the
  same document, an earlier draft, or the separate June 2, 1962 "Memorandum of
  Organization" the deep-research report cited (a document distinct from Miller's
  August 1962 "Considerations" memo).

## What this collection does NOT have

The "Individual Council Chairs' Papers, 1988-2002" series only covers five chairs
from 1988 onward (Karl Taeuber, William Flanigan, Carole Shammas, Halliman
Winsborough, Ann Green). No dedicated Marvick folder exists under his own name.
Anything from his 1966-67 chairmanship would be inside the general Council meeting
minutes, not filed separately.

## Access

Portions of the collection are in offsite storage and require prior notification.
Records are open for research; restrictions only cover personnel, student, patient,
and executive-officer records, none of which apply to the folders above.
