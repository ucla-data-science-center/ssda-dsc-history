## Real discrepancy #1: when was UC Carpentries founded, and by whom?

- **Tim's SOPA** (2023-2025 review): "I **founded** UC Carpentries **in 2020** and built it into
  a formal 10-campus UC network... As Convener and Chair (2024-present)... the network delivered
  28 workshops reaching 2,896 learners: 1,240 in 2023, 937 in 2024, and 719 in 2025."
- **Tim's Data Summary** (same review cycle, different document): "**Co-founder** & Convener, UC
  Carpentries... **2018** – present... Workshops delivered: 37 (2022–2025). Total attendees:
  1,495 (2024–2025)."

**Resolved, per Tim (2026-08-09): these are two different entities, not a contradiction.** Tim
started **UCLA Carpentries** (the campus-level program) in **2018**. He **co-founded UC
Carpentries** (the systemwide, multi-campus network) in **2020**, driven by COVID — the pandemic
created the need for coordinated, remote, cross-campus training that a single-campus program
couldn't meet alone. The SOPA's "founded... 2020" language was imprecise (it's the co-founded
systemwide network, not a sole founding), and both documents were compressing the
UCLA-Carpentries-to-UC-Carpentries lineage into a single line. Use: **UCLA Carpentries, founded
2018; UC Carpentries, co-founded 2020 (COVID-driven expansion)**.

Workshop/learner counts (28 workshops/2,896 learners in the SOPA vs. 37 workshops/1,495
attendees in the Data Summary) are still not fully reconciled — plausibly different scopes
(UC Carpentries alone vs. UCLA+UC Carpentries combined; different date windows) given the
entity distinction above, but not confirmed. Use the SOPA's explicitly-scoped "UC Carpentries,
2023-2025 review period, 28 workshops, 2,896 learners" as the safer citation if one number is
needed, and don't state the Data Summary's total as directly comparable without asking further.
