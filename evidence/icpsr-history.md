Pulled 2026-07-07 via Wayback Machine (live page 403s to scripted fetches; content from the
2025 capture, © Regents of the University of Michigan). Institutional self-history: use for
dates and growth numbers, pair with Shankar et al. 2016 for analysis.

## Founding claim (exact phrasing)

"ICPSR was founded in 1962 by political scientist Warren E. Miller to share scientific data.
Before this, scientific data from all disciplines were closely held by their developers and
creators. Since then, ICPSR has expanded the impact of social and behavioral research, acting
as a global leader in data stewardship and providing rich data resources and responsive
educational opportunities for the research data community."

Note the vocabulary: ICPSR describes its own 60-year arc as **"data stewardship"** plus
education. The paper's terms are the incumbent archive's terms.

- Topical archives accreted continuously from 1962 (Survey Data Archive) through 2022
  (Social Media Archive, SOMAR) - the consortium model absorbs new object types decade
  after decade. That is a 60-year federated-stewardship precedent with a working revenue
  model (membership), directly relevant to §5's federation design and §7's sustainability.
- Current holdings: **8,880 on-demand studies / 72,548 datasets / 236,926 files** (plus
  restricted). UCLA was a founding member (1962, per the 2017 deck and slide 5 lineage).

## Discrepancy flag

Tim's 2017 deck (slide 11 notes) compared SSDA's ~600 datasets to "ICPSR approx. 9,000 data
sets." ICPSR's own current count is ~8,880 **studies** but ~72,500 **datasets**. The 2017
comparison was almost certainly studies-to-datasets loose usage; if the ~600 vs ~9,000
comparison is used in the paper, say "studies" or "collections," not "datasets."
