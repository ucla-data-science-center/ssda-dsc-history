## Tim Dennis SOPA (2023-2025 review period) — research enabled

**UCLA Dataverse**: 1,603 datasets, 54,655 files, 142,300 downloads, 312 registered depositing
users (up from 15 files in 2019). Self-service model, no proportional staffing increase.
(Note: this exact figure is already logged and disputed in
`dsc-evidence-registry/claims/dataverse-dataset-count.yaml` against a conflicting 377-dataset
figure from `dsc-stats-reports`' live dashboard — this SOPA read confirms, doesn't resolve, that
existing dispute.)

**Carceral Ecologies** (PI: Nicholas Shapiro) — a 2024 UCLA Public Impact Research Award winner.
Maps carceral infrastructure including aerial surveillance of incarcerated communities. DSC
provided R server hosting, multi-year technical support, and a code optimization that cut
processing time from 318 minutes to 2 minutes. Drew national media coverage (LA Times, *The New
Yorker*, Bloomberg) and generated pilot data behind a $5 million Dana Center grant. Established
a dedicated sub-dataverse, published five datasets on carceral infrastructure. Shapiro's quote,
per Tim's SOPA: DSC "turned my need to pivot into something more valuable to the city and the
university."

**BioCritical Studies Lab** (PI: Terence Keel) — the second 2024 Public Impact Research Award
winner DSC supported. DataSquad integrated crowdsourced datasets on deaths in custody, built
statistical analyses and national-scale visualizations, delivered tools the lab now runs
independently. Assistant Director Grace Sosa: DSC is "one of the most impactful resources I have
engaged with in my time as a student and staff researcher at UCLA."

**Redivis** (restricted/sensitive data platform): selected and licensed by Tim, launched January
2023, closing a real institutional gap (before this, UCLA had no compliant environment for
restricted multi-terabyte national datasets — no voter-file access, no secure merge environment).
Grew from 5 workflows / 444 GB (first year) to 2.9 PB processed in 2024. Enabled a UCLA Political
Science research group (Straus, Thompson, Ferrer, Geyn) to publish in *American Political Science
Review*, *PNAS*, *British Journal of Political Science*, and *AEJ: Applied Economics* — top-tier
venues. Doctoral researcher Graham Straus, quoted at FORCE11 2024: "What happens when I leave
UCLA? Will people know how the dataset was constructed? Yes!"

**Security/resilience**: in early 2025, AI-driven bots attacked UCLA Dataverse (Jan-June),
mass-downloading research data for apparent model training while evading IP-blocking by rotating
across thousands of addresses. Tim diagnosed it and deployed Anubis (open-source reverse-proxy
bot filter, github.com/TecharoHQ/anubis) — contained by June 2025. Real, recent, citable
infrastructure-resilience event.

**DataSquad**: 1,175 documented service interactions this review period; 190 direct student
consultations. Placement rate on graduation: McKinsey & Company, Amazon, AIG, Stanford (100%,
per SOPA).

## Tim Dennis — intellectual contributions to the profession

- **IMLS grant** (RE-252335-OLS-22, $237,839 — already logged in
  `evidence/powell-gifts-and-imls-grant.md`): delivered **15** peer-reviewed lessons against a
  proposed 14. Treated curriculum as a first-class research output — DOI assignment via Zenodo,
  CRediT taxonomy, ORCID integration, Citation File Format metadata. Tim authored *Introduction
  to Computational Thinking*, *Introduction to R*, and a rewrite of *Introduction to Python*
  among the lessons. Organized and taught Collaborative Lesson Development training for all 28
  co-authors across 14 institutions.
- **UC Carpentries** — Tim **founded it in 2020**, built it into a formal 10-campus UC network
  (plus CSU/UCOP participation) with shared governance. Convener and Chair, 2024-present: 43
  active instructors/helpers, 28 workshops delivered this review period reaching 2,896 learners
  (1,240 in 2023, 937 in 2024, 719 in 2025).
- **Library Carpentry Curriculum Advisory Committee (LCCAC)**, Chair since 2022: formalized
  adoption pathways bringing 6 new lessons (including the IMLS-grant-produced DMP 101 lesson)
  into official worldwide Library Carpentry curriculum.
- **The Carpentries** (the parent organization): Instructor Trainer since 2016; certified as a
  Lesson Development Trainer, 2024; served on the inaugural Trainers Leadership Committee
  (through 2023), co-authoring its bylaws; edited the complete revision of The Carpentries
  Instructor Training curriculum.
- **UC OSPO Network**: Co-Principal Investigator on the Sloan Foundation's $1.85M multi-campus
  grant; serves on the OSPO Leadership Group (the 6-campus governing body). Built the Education
  Working Group from scratch (personas, metadata standards, search facets, lesson inventory).
  Co-organized UC Open Summit 2025 (~125 participants, platinum sponsorship from major research
  funders).
- **Publication**: co-authored a chapter in ACRL's *Data Culture* documenting the DataSquad
  model — the model has since been adopted internationally via the DataSquad International
  network.
- **Research Data Working Group**: Chair of the Data Services Subcommittee, member of the RDWG
  Steering Committee; co-authored a research-infrastructure roadmap guiding UCLA institutional
  investment decisions.
- Staff-nominated for UCLA's AMG 2025 Excellence in Leadership Award.

## Doug Daniels — research enabled and professional contributions (SOPA, 2022-2023 + 2026-2027 SOR)

**Confirmed via his 2026-2027 SOR**: operates and sustains UCLA's point-cloud/3D visualization
infrastructure at **pointcloud.ucla.edu, built on Potree** — resolves the earlier uncertainty in
`evidence/doug-daniels-and-2018-continuity.md` about whether Potree specifically was involved.

**Named research projects, with real collaborators and outputs**:
- **Easter Island Moai statuette scanning** (Jan-March 2022), with **Jo Anne Van Tilburg**,
  Director of the Rock Art Archive at the Cotsen Institute of Archaeology. Produced high-
  resolution scans of 1/10-scale Moai models to supply measurements researchers couldn't
  otherwise obtain from partially buried/broken originals. **Accepted for publication**: a
  chapter in *Te Moai Rapa Nui: The Statues of Easter Island: An Illustrated Atlas* (Cotsen
  Institute of Archaeology Press, ~500pp, expected publication ~early 2025) — Doug's scanning
  work and data are the subject of Chapter 9, "Moai Anatomy and Ahu Sequence."
- **Stein Eye Institute** (Spring 2022): 3D-scanned conjunctiva tissue from cadavers with
  teaching faculty and graduate students, intended to support machine-learning-based automated
  tissue measurement.
- **Anatomy Lab** (Fall 2022), with History faculty and Dr. Anthony Friscia (Director, Cluster
  Program): scanned a human arm across dissection stages; now used in History of Medicine
  coursework taught by Prof. Chien-Ling Liu Zeleny — teaching material otherwise available only
  one quarter per year.
- **Drone/LiDAR mapping kit** acquired late spring/early summer 2023 (drone, RTK base station,
  LiDAR + photogrammetry payloads); Doug obtained FAA UAS pilot certification and built dedicated
  3D workstation infrastructure. Used on two overseas archaeological projects: an ongoing
  Angkor Wat temple-mapping project (Cambodia/Philippines), and the **Mosfell Valley, Iceland**
  survey (summer 2023, with a Cotsen Institute doctoral student — already logged via UCLA
  Newsroom coverage).
- Advised **Zelin Deng** (Materials Science graduate student) on a Capstone project applying
  non-visible-spectrum texture data to 3D models.
- Ten guest lectures in one review period alone, across Art, History, Archaeology, Digital
  Humanities, Engineering, and Biochemistry.
