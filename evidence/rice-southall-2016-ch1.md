## Why this matters for the paper

- **Pushes the lineage back to 1957.** The wave history in `timeline.md` started in the
  1980s; this table documents an entire earlier wave of institutional data stewardship,
  three decades before RDM and five before "data science."
- **UCLA Data Services, 1961.** The institution Tim now directs (as the Data Science
  Center) appears in the founding cohort — fourth-oldest in the table, second-oldest US
  data library/service after Berkeley. This is the strongest possible version of the
  "libraries have done this work for decades" claim: not decades, sixty-five years,
  documented by an independent source.
- **Carleton University 1965** is a nice resonance with the Carleton College DataSquad
  origin (different Carleton, do not conflate; flag if anyone reads it as the same).
- **The distinction between "data centre" and "data library/service"** in the table's Type
  column is an early version of the paper's coordination-vs-stewardship distinction:
  archives accepted custody, libraries/services provided access and support.

## Caveats

- Data points are Martinez's compilation as reprinted; a couple of dates read oddly
  (DANS as an organization dates to 2005; 1964 is its Steinmetz Archive predecessor, which
  the "Other names" column signals). Cite the table for the wave, verify any single date
  before making a claim depend on it.
- Chapter prose (the "responding to research innovation" framing) still to pull for
  quoting; only the table is in hand.
