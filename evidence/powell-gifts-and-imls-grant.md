# Norman Powell's gifts, and the IMLS open science grant

Added 2026-08-09 at Tim's direction, researched live rather than taken on faith.

## Norman Powell — multiple distinct gifts, only one precisely dated

Norman Powell, UCLA alumnus, was a long-time UCLA Library donor. Per UCLA Library's own
retrospective on him [@ucla-library-powell-obit — note: not yet added to references.bib, add
before citing in history.qmd], he made at least five distinct gifts/contributions, only one of
which carries a specific date and dollar figure in the sources checked:

1. **December 15, 2016 — $5 million.** Endowed the University Librarian position itself (a
   dean-level role overseeing all eight UCLA campus libraries), named the "Norman and Armena
   Powell University Librarian." Confirmed via UCLA Newsroom press release, dated and sourced
   precisely. This is **not** the data-scientist/DataSquad funding — a separate, earlier set of
   gifts covers that (next item).
2. **Data scientist position** (date not given in sources checked) — funded as part of his
   early support for UCLA's Sustainable LA Grand Challenge, per UCLA Library's retrospective.
   Plausibly connects to Leigh Phan's programmer-to-data-scientist role transition (see People
   and stewardship) — not yet confirmed as the same funding line, worth asking Tim directly.
3. **A deep learning machine and other Data Science Center space enhancements** in the Charles
   E. Young Research Library (date not given) — plausibly the hardware behind the "deep
   learning work with Leigh Phan" Tim described; not yet confirmed as the same machine.
4. **DataSquad seed funding** (date not given) — the founding funding for the UCLA Library
   DataSquad, the undergraduate data-science-consulting service.
5. **Barbara and Willard Morgan Photographs and Papers acquisition support** (date not given) —
   also matched other donors' gifts toward the same acquisition.

**Caution**: items 2-4 are undated in every source checked so far (UCLA Library's retrospective
article gives no years for them). Don't assign them a specific year without another source —
ask Tim, or check UCLA Library's DataSquad program materials/annual reports directly.

## IMLS grant — dated, numbered, and precise

**RE-252335-OLS-22**, awarded FY2022, **$237,839**, to UCLA Library, for "Lessons for Librarians
in Open Science Principles and Methods." Led by the DSC. Two-year project (2023-2024): a
competitive process awarding lesson authors $5,000 each to develop open-science curriculum
modules for library professionals, via two virtual summer schools. Outcome: 14-15 open,
modular lessons; per the Carpentries' own 2026 call for community pilots, 27 expert authors
across 14 teams produced 15 new lesson repositories, with the remaining 14 lessons at Alpha
status as of that call.

This is a distinct, already-completed/awarded grant — different from `ospo_datasquad_grant`,
which (per that project's own registry notes) is a separate, still-in-proposal IMLS submission
targeting the FY27 cycle. Don't conflate the two in the history.
