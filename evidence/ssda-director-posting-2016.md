# "Director of the UCLA Libraries Social Science Data Archive" posting (2016)

Source: `ssda-director-posting-2016.pdf`, printed 2026-09-25 from the IASSIST jobs repository: https://iassistdata.org/jobs-repository/2016-06-27-00411/ (posted 2016-06-27). Tim Dennis applied from this posting and started 2017-04-17.

## Key facts (quoted from the posting)

* Title: "Director of the UCLA Libraries Social Science Data Archive."
* "The Social Science Data Archive (SSDA), in operation since 1961, is a recognized leader in the management of quantitative social science research data and represents UCLA in local, state, national and international professional communities."
* The role covers "planning, coordinating, and deploying research data services directed at supporting the research lifecycle."
* Four areas: data curation and preservation; consultation and services; technical infrastructure; education, awareness and community building. "The initial focus of this position will be to contribute to the development of the data repository."
* "Oversees SSDA operational and administrative activities, with the goal of creating innovative and sustainable data curation services."
* Qualifications include knowledge of repository design and the OAIS reference model (ISO 14721).

## Use

The Library's own description, two years after the 2014 transfer, of how the archive would be led and what the role covered. Pairs with `ssda-library-mou.md` and `library-era-2014-present.md`.
