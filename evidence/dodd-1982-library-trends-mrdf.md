# Dodd 1982, Library Trends — UCLA cited as an early MRDF library case

Sue A. Dodd, "Toward Integration of Catalog Records on Social Science Machine-Readable Data
Files Into Existing Bibliographic Utilities: A Commentary." *Library Trends* 30(3), Winter 1982,
335-361. (file: `librarytrendsv30i3d_opt.pdf`)

General topic: national history of library bibliographic control over machine-readable data
files (MRDF), 1970-1982 — ALA's Descriptive Cataloging Committee subcommittee on MRDF (formed
Jan 1970, final report Jan 1976, fed into AACR2 chapter 9 in 1978), IASSIST's cataloging
feasibility project, ICPSR's 1979 NEH-funded automated cataloging system, the MARC format for
MRDF (1979-1981). Mostly not UCLA-specific — cited here for one direct hit.

## The UCLA reference (p.341)

> "In other cases, like the University of California at Los Angeles, it was the automated 1970
> census records that became the first MRDF to be included as part of a library's collection."

Footnoted (ref. 10) to: **Rowe, Judith S., and Ryan, Mary. "Library Service from Numerical Data
Bases: The 1970 Census as a Paradigm." *College & Research Libraries* 35 (Jan. 1974): 7-15.**

## Why this matters

- **Contemporaneous, not retrospective.** Rowe & Ryan 1974 was written at the time, unlike
  everything else in this project's evidence base so far, which is either Tim's own later memory
  (2017 deck) or a 2015-2018 retrospective digitization/re-deposit artifact (Dataverse, ICPSR).
- **Independent partial corroboration of a previously-unverified claim.** The 2017 deck's speaker
  notes state "in 1970 the Archive and the Research Library jointly hired a programmer/tech
  consultant for the 1970 Census" — flagged in `evidence/ucla-ssda-lineage-2017-deck.md` as
  tracing to Jamie Jamison's memory, "verify against a written source before putting a claim's
  full weight on it." This doesn't confirm the specific hire, but it independently confirms the
  same underlying event — UCLA Library's early, nationally-noted involvement with 1970 census
  MRDF — from a source outside Tim's own institutional memory, written six years before "SSDA"
  even existed as a name (per the 1977 ISSR renaming).
- **Predates the archive's own naming.** This 1970-74 activity happened under whatever the
  pre-SSDA, pre-ISSR unit was (likely the Political Behavior Archive / early Survey Research
  Center era, per the 1964 SRC formation already documented) — a genuine pre-1977 data point,
  which the project has had almost none of until now.
