# Phan et al. 2021 — DSC-era program evidence, and a major lead

Phan, Leigh, Labou, Stephanie, Foster, Erin, Ali, Ibraheem. "A Model for Data Ethics Instruction
for Non-Experts." *IASSIST Quarterly* 46(4), 2021, pp. 1-15. DOI: 10.29173/iq1028. (file:
`IQ46_4_1028,+Ali+and+Phan,+Manuscript_12272022.pdf`)

Added by Tim, 2026-08-09 — Leigh (Phan) co-authored.

## What it documents (2018-present DSC era)

Leigh Phan (Data Scientist, UCLA Library DSC) and Ibraheem Ali (Sciences Data Librarian, UCLA
Library DSC) co-organized, with UC San Diego and UC Berkeley library colleagues, two cross-UC
data ethics workshops as part of UC Love Data Week: "Ethical Considerations in Data" (2021) and
"Data Ethics & Justice" (2022). Real attendance/demographic data included (Figures 1-3):
2021 workshop drew 40.5% Social Sciences/Humanities, 21.6% Health/Physical Sciences, 37.8%
Other/Interdisciplinary; 2022 workshop had 57% staff registrants, with Health Sciences (21),
Social Sciences (19), and Libraries (19) as the top disciplinary areas. This is real, citable
program evidence for the DSC era's instructional-service work — the kind of material the
README's "2018-present DSC era" gap says exists but "not yet assembled into a standalone
historical narrative."

## The major lead: Tim's own book chapter

Reference list includes: **Dennis et al. 2021. "From a Data Archive to Data Science: Supporting
Current Research." In Herndon, J. (Ed.), *Data science in the library: tools and strategies for
supporting data-driven research and instruction* (1st ed., pp. 99-109). Facet Publishing.**

This is explicitly framed (by its own title) as an account of the SSDA→DSC transition, published
in an edited scholarly volume, authored by Tim. It has not been read/pulled into this project's
evidence base yet. Given the project's whole purpose is reconstructing exactly that transition,
this chapter should likely be one of the first sources consulted directly, not just cited
secondhand through Phan et al.'s reference list — it may already contain a considered, published
account of facts this project has been reconstructing piecemeal from decks, MOUs, and Dataverse
metadata.
