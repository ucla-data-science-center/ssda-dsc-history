# SSDA / DSC Institutional History

An intellectual history of UCLA's social science data archive lineage — from the Political Behavior Archive (founded 1961 by Dwaine Marvick) through ISSR stewardship, the 2014 transfer to the Library, and the 2018 formation of the Data Science Center — to the present.

Purpose: memorialize the archive's institutional history in its own right, independent of any grant, advocacy, or reorg document that happens to cite parts of it.

## Known gaps (the actual research still to do)
- **1977–2014, the ISSR era** — substantially filled by `evidence/deep-research-report.md` (2026-07-31): three named directors (Freeman late-70s/~1980, Brewer 1983, Sears 1993–2008), a dozen named operational staff, and a three-subperiod structure (ISSR → renamed Institute for Social Research ~2009–10 → standalone SSDA ~2011 onward, pre-Library-transfer). Remaining holes: exact reporting line 2011–2014, who directed the renamed ISR (no name found for 2009–11), the formal ISR dissolution date/authority, and Treiman's precise relationship to ISSR (see connective-tissue note below). None of this is independently verified past the report's own citations yet.
- **Pre-1977 connective tissue (new, 2026-07-31)** — the deep-research report surfaces a real conflict: all three existing sources (both 2017 decks, the Dataverse About page) say the archive "merged with"/"joined" the Survey Research Center in **1964 under Eve Fielder**, but a Fielder obituary the report cites says she became SRC's **first director in 1976** — the same year the report says SRC was incorporated into ISSR. So either (a) SRC existed from 1964 under a different founding director and Fielder took over later, or (b) the "1964 + Fielder" framing in the internal decks compresses two separate events. This directly bears on the 1961→1962→1964→1977 chain Tim built the project around (PBA founding → ICPSR founding member → SRC merger → ISSR), so it needs a documentary check before publication, not a silent pick between sources.
- **1977's institutional home — four internal sources, four different framings.** Both 2017 decks say the archive came under **ISSR** in 1977. The Dataverse About page names **Treiman** as the 1977 framework's designer with no institutional home stated. The `data-sci-libraries_chapter_edits.md` book chapter says SSDA was created in 1977 "as part of the **Survey Research Center**," no ISSR mention. The deep-research report's external evidence (UCLA General Catalog 1977–78) supports ISSR as the actual named parent, with SRC as "an integral component" under it, not the other way around — so the book chapter's SRC-only framing looks like the one to correct, but that's worth confirming against the catalog directly before treating it as settled.
- **ICPSR "8 founding members" claim — reopened 2026-07-31.** UCLA's founding-member status is confirmed (both Austin's 22-institution count and Miller's dated 21-institution list include UCLA). The "8" headcount looked wrong until Tim reported that Maggie Levenstein, ICPSR's current director, independently used the same "8" figure during a UCLA visit. The Bentley finding aid holds a folder titled "Committee of Eight, Correspondence and Minutes, 1962–1963" — a plausible referent for "8" that's a different thing entirely from the 21/22-institution membership roster. Not confirmed. Cheapest next step: ask Levenstein directly what she meant.
- **Marvick's claimed ICPSR founding role** — still unresolved, but more plausible than before. Marvick isn't in ICPSR's 1962 founding council list, Austin's essay, or Miller's own 1962 memo, and his documented ICPSR involvement (governing board, then Council chair 1966–67) starts in 1964, after founding. But Eulau's 1999 memoir (2026-07-31) establishes Marvick and Miller were close personal friends a full decade before ICPSR existed, together at Michigan's SRC in the early 1950s. Real context for "through his efforts," not proof of it.
- **Newsletters** — deep-research report found none digitized in any public index (UCLA General Catalog, eScholarship, Internet Archive, HathiTrust, Google Books, UCLA Digital Library all checked). This is a negative search result, not proof none exist — the physical-location inquiry (University Archives, former staff) is still the live lead.
- **People for oral history** — the director list (Freeman, Brewer, Sears) plus operational staff (Eve Fielder, Madeleine/Madelyn De Maria, Rita Engelhardt, Donald Witzke, Michael Greenwell, Tana Wong, and others) gives a real candidate list now — availability/living status not yet checked for any of them.
- **UCLA institutional records** — University Archives may hold founding documents, ISSR records, or early annual reports not yet pulled; the deep-research report worked entirely from public web sources, not archives.
- ~~**2018–present (DSC era)**~~ — largely resolved 2026-07-31 by `evidence/data-sci-libraries_chapter_edits.md`, a full book-chapter draft already covering this ground (2014 Library transfer → 2018 DSC rebrand → Carpentries → Dataverse → staffing → future goals). Remaining work here is adaptation/excerpting for the history piece, not new research.
