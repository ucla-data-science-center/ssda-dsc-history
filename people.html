NoteLibbie Stephenson: the single best-documented witness to this whole era

Her UCLA tenure runs almost exactly the length of the ISSR era itself: started around 1977, the same year the archive was formally reconstituted as a named ISSR unit, and continued through the 2014 Library transfer and two more years, retiring in 2016. BA Fine Arts (UC Riverside, 1975), MLS (UCLA, 1977). IASSIST President 1995–97. ICPSR Council 1996–2000. Won the 2007 William H. Flanigan Award for Distinguished Service as an ICPSR Official Representative, and a 2016 IASSIST achievement award shortly before retiring. See evidence/stephenson-icpsr-sources.md. Correction: an earlier note on this site said 2014; that was wrong. Two independent primary sources (the retirement article’s own date stamp, and its reference to the 2016 IASSIST conference in Bergen, Norway, confirmed independently) put it at 2016. She’s very likely the strongest oral-history candidate on this entire page: unlike Marvick or the ISSR-era directors, she retired only a decade ago.

Notes

  • Most pre-2014 names beyond Stephenson and Jamison come from a single deep-research pass and have not been independently verified against primary documents. See Sources & Open Questions.

  • Several 2014–present entries above rest partly on Tim’s own direct recollection as a participant, not yet cross-checked against a document. That’s a different, valid kind of source (oral testimony from someone who was there), not a lesser one, but it’s marked separately from publicly documented facts so the two don’t blur together.
