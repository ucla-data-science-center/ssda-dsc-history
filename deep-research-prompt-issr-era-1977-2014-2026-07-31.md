# Deep-research request: UCLA's Social Science Data Archive under ISSR, 1977–2014

## Project context

I'm writing a citable institutional history of UCLA's social science data archive
lineage for a public website. The archive started in 1961 as the Political Behavior
Archive (founded by political science professor Dwaine Marvick), became a founding
member of ICPSR in 1962, joined UCLA's Survey Research Center (SRC) under Eve Fielder
in 1964, and in 1977 became the **Social Science Data Archive (SSDA)** under UCLA's
**Institute for Social Science Research (ISSR)**. The Survey Research Center closed
around 2003–2009, after which ISSR shifted toward grant support and the Archive
continued as a standalone unit inside UCLA's Social Sciences Division (SSD). In 2014
(MOU executed August 2015), SSDA transferred to the UCLA Library, becoming the seed
of what is now the Data Science Center (DSC, formed 2018).

**The gap I need help closing:** the 1977–2014 stretch (~37 years) under ISSR
stewardship is currently a single unsourced sentence in my draft. I have no
directors, no staff beyond the two people known to have carried over at the 2014
handoff, no description of what the archive did or how its role changed over nearly
four decades. Everything before 1977 and after 2014 is corroborated by multiple
independent sources; this stretch has none. I need public-web evidence to start
filling it in, before I invest time contacting UCLA University Archives or former
staff directly.

## What I already know (do not re-derive — use as anchors/keywords, not open questions)

  Behavior Archive in 1961.
- **Eve Fielder** — directed the Survey Research Center UCLA joined in 1964.
- **Institute for Social Science Research (ISSR)** — UCLA unit that took over the
  archive in 1977; unclear whether ISSR still exists today in any form, or was
  absorbed/dissolved/renamed.
- **Social Sciences Division (SSD)** — UCLA division the archive lived in as a
  standalone unit after SRC closed (~2003/2009) until the 2014 Library transfer.
- **Elizabeth Stephenson** (Librarian) and **Jina/Jamie Jamison** (staff, UC title
  "PA 3" — Programmer/Analyst 3) — the two staff named in the 2014 MOU as continuing
  "with no break in service," meaning they were SSDA/ISSR staff immediately before
  the 2014 transfer. Jamison is still DSC staff as of 2026.
- **Alessandro Duranti** — Dean of Social Sciences, signed the MOU 2015-08-10.
- **Virginia Steel** — UCLA University Librarian, signed the MOU 2015-08-04.
- SSDA held ~600 curated datasets by 2017 (per an internal deck — not necessarily a
  public source, don't expect to corroborate this number).
- ICPSR itself was founded 1962 by Warren E. Miller; UCLA was a founding member
  institution. UCLA's own Harvard Dataverse "About" page (dataverse.harvard.edu/dataverse/ssda_ucla)
  claims UCLA was one of **8** founding members — this number is unverified and conflicts with
  what little I have on ICPSR's founding cohort size, so treat it as a claim to check, not a fact.
- **Donald Treiman** — UCLA sociology professor. The same Dataverse About page states he

  have designed the framework as an ISSR-affiliated director, or ISSR may have administratively
  housed a framework he designed in some other capacity — I don't know which, and need this
  resolved rather than silently merged.
